Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

PulseCircle (“we”, “us”) lets you share selected Apple Health information with people you choose. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Information you provide

When you create an account with Sign in with Apple, we receive an account identifier and, if you allow it, your name and email address. Apple may hide your email behind a private relay address. You can also add a username, bio, country, and profile photo.

Health information

With your permission, PulseCircle reads health data from Apple Health on your iPhone. This can include steps, walking distance, active energy, exercise minutes, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, respiratory rate, blood oxygen, sleep, stand hours, weight, body temperature, and blood pressure.

PulseCircle reads this data. It does not write data into Apple Health. Health data stays on your device until you turn on sharing. When sharing is on, a summary of the metrics you allow is uploaded so the friends you approved can see it. Friends only receive the metrics you leave enabled. You can pause or revoke access for any person in Privacy. Blocking or removing a friend stops further sharing with that person.

How the service works

We store your profile, friend connections, sharing permissions, health summaries, check-ins, and notification token in Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, and Cloud Messaging). Firebase processes this data to run the app: sign-in, friend search, delivery of shared health summaries, profile photos, and push notifications.

We use this information to operate PulseCircle, show shared health to people you authorize, send notifications you opt into, and keep the service reliable. We do not sell personal information or health information. We do not use your health data for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Sharing

We share health summaries only with PulseCircle users you have accepted as friends and for whom an active sharing permission exists. We also share data with service providers that host the app, currently Google Firebase, and when the law requires it. Those providers process data on our instructions to provide the service.

Retention and deletion

We keep account and health summaries while your account is open. You can delete your account in Settings. Deletion removes your profile, friends, requests, and stored health summaries from our systems. Apple and Firebase may keep limited records where the law requires it. Revoking Health access in the iOS Health app stops new reads from Apple Health.

Children

PulseCircle is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly create accounts for them.
